Privacy Policy for My Budget

Last updated: 10 September 2026

The short version: My Budget collects nothing. It has no account, no analytics, and no advertising. Everything you enter stays in a database on your own device, and the released app does not have permission to use the internet at all.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the My Budget mobile application ("the app"), an offline personal expense tracker. It describes what happens to the information you enter into the app.

Information collected about you

None. The app does not ask for, gather, or transmit any personal or device information. Specifically, it does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials — the app has no sign-up or login of any kind.
  • Your location.
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or usage analytics.
  • Contacts, photos, files, or any other content on your device.
  • Crash reports or diagnostic telemetry.

What the app stores, and where

The expenses, categories, and settings you enter are written to a private SQLite database inside the app's own storage area on your device. Android isolates this storage so that other apps cannot read it.

This information never leaves your device. It is not uploaded to any server, and the developer has no access to it and no ability to view, retrieve, or recover it.

Network access

The released version of the app does not declare the Android INTERNET permission. Without that permission the operating system will not allow the app to open a network connection, so it is not technically capable of sending your data anywhere.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising networks, no analytics or attribution SDKs, no crash reporting services, and no social media integrations. No information is sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone, because none is collected.

Device backups

Android may include the app's data in the automatic device backup associated with your Google account, which is a feature of the operating system rather than of this app. Those backups are governed by Google's privacy policy and are controlled entirely by you in your device settings, where automatic backup can be turned off. The developer has no access to them.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored locally, you are always in control of it. Clearing the app's storage from your device settings, or uninstalling the app, permanently removes all of the data it holds. There is nothing stored elsewhere that would need to be deleted, and no request needs to be made to anyone.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and since it collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this policy will be updated and the date at the top revised. Material changes will be reflected in the app's store listing.
