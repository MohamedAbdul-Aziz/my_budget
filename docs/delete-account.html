Delete your My Budget account

My Budget, by Mohamed Mahmoud

Deleting your account permanently removes the account (your email address and password) and every record in your cloud backup. Nothing is kept afterwards. The records stored on your phone are not affected.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open My Budget and open Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. The account and its cloud backup are deleted straight away.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email zmohamedzizo827@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my My Budget account. The account and all of its cloud data are deleted within 30 days, and you get a reply once it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your email address and password.
  • All backed-up transactions, categories, budgets, settings, recurring payments, people, debts, settlements and their edit history.

To also remove the data on your phone, clear the app's storage in your device settings or uninstall the app.
