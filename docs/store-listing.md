app name 30 characters, short description 80, full description 4000.

Everything here matches what the app actually does — the month breakdown

## English (default listing)

### App name

My Budget

### Short description

Simple offline expense tracker. Your data never leaves your phone.

### Full description

My Budget is a personal expense tracker that works entirely offline.

There is no sign-up, no account, and no internet permission. Everything you record is stored in a private database on your own phone — never uploaded, never sold, never seen by anyone but you.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Record an expense in seconds: amount, category, date and an optional note
• See what you spent this month, broken down by category and ranked by spend
• Browse previous months and what each one cost you
• Create your own categories with a colour and an icon, or use the built-in ones
• Undo a deletion straight away if you remove something by mistake
• Set the currency symbol you prefer

HOME SCREEN WIDGET
Add the Quick Expense widget to see this month's total at a glance. It keeps shortcuts to the categories you use most — tap one, type the amount, and the expense is saved without opening the app.

ENGLISH AND ARABIC
Fully translated, including category names, dates and number formatting. It follows your phone's language automatically, or you can choose one yourself. Right-to-left layout is fully supported.

LIGHT AND DARK
Follows your system theme, or set light or dark yourself.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
The released app does not request the internet permission at all, so it is not technically able to send your data anywhere. No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no crash reporting. Uninstalling removes everything it stored.

لا يوجد تسجيل دخول ولا حساب ولا صلاحية إنترنت. كل ما تسجّله يُحفظ في قاعدة بيانات خاصة على هاتفك — لا يُرفع إلى أي مكان، ولا يُباع، ولا يراه أحد غيرك.
